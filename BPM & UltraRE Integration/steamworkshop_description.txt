[h2] Integration For Better Politics Mods and Ultra Historical Research and Education[/h2]
Compatible with vanilla 1.13 version and BPM 2.5
This mod provides compatibility and integration for two affordmentionned mods
[list]
This mod changes some of modifiers from ministry of education to be more in line with UltraRE balance
[*] Reduced effects of most ministers
[*]	Added labor consciousness modifiers for conservative and leftist ministers
[*] Added fervor modifiers for army and fascists
[*] Added new natlib minister modifier
[*] Rebalance communist and anarachists minister effects
[/list]

[list]
Officers qualifications  were reworked to be based on active officer law:
[*] Noble officers will significantly increase qualification for aristocrats.
[*] Noble officers will now significantly decrease non aristocrat qualifications to become officers rather than peasant levies
[*] Purchased officers will make pop qualification to become officer be depending on wealth 
[*] Political Officers will increase bureaucrats qualification to become officers and decrease qualification of every other pop type
[*] Professional Officers will  increase servicemen qualifications even if they are not deployed
[*] Elected officers will significantly increase servicemen qualifications even if they are not deployed, and significantly reduce qualification of aristocrats
